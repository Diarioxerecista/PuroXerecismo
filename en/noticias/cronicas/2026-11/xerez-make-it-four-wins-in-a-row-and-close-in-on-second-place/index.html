Newsroom — 15 November 2026

Xerez CD made it four wins in a row on Sunday by beating Atlético Antoniano 2-0 at the Pedro Garrido, on matchday 11 of Group IV of Segunda RFEF. Abel Segovia's side settled the game in the first half, with an Isma penalty in the 23rd minute and a goal from Mario Peregrina in the 44th. There were two red cards for second yellows: the visitors' Pedro Melli in the 21st minute and Moi in the 50th.

With this win, Xerez are fifth. They are level on points with Betis Deportivo, but ahead on overall goal difference, and two points behind second place. The leaders, Recreativo de Huelva, are three points above and have their game this matchday against Sevilla Atlético pending, postponed because of their participation in the Copa Federación final.

Changes to the line-up

Segovia made changes to the line-up. Guille Campos, one booking away from a ban after four yellow cards, started on the bench and Víctor López played at right-back. Fali returned to the centre of defence alongside Moi. Antoniano, coached by Lolo Ortiz, left former Xerez player Ricky among the substitutes.

Xerez went for the game and found their main route to goal down the left, through Zeki and Chacartegui. In the 4th minute, Isma reached the byline, but nobody got on the end of his cut-back. The home side won three corners in the first twelve minutes. Antoniano only threatened once, when Carlos Pantoja cut out a square pass from Gálvez inside the box.

The penalty that changed the game

The key moment came in the 21st minute. Víctor López crossed from the right and Pedro Melli held Chuma as he was about to shoot. The Extremadura referee Jayro Muñoz García took his time, but awarded the penalty and showed the visiting defender a second yellow card. Isma converted it with a central shot at mid-height, while goalkeeper Matías Árbol dived to his right.

With an extra man, Xerez had the ball and got forward with ease, with a very involved Mario Peregrina, although they lacked precision in the final metres. Antoniano sat back with five and even six players on the edge of their box. In the 44th minute, Chuma won the ball in midfield, went into the box, beat his marker and laid it off to Peregrina, who got ahead of his defender to beat Matías Árbol. Before half-time, Moi was booked for a foul from which Nando's free kick went over the bar.

Ten against ten after the break

Lolo Ortiz brought on Divino and Ricky on the right after the break. The first chance fell to Xerez: a one-on-one for Chuma that Matías Árbol turned away. In the 50th minute, Moi received a second yellow card for a foul on Gálvez on the edge of the box, and Ricky Castro put the free kick over the bar. Segovia rebuilt the back four by bringing on Manu Galán for Zeki in the 52nd minute. Four minutes later, Matías Árbol was called into action again by a Chuma free kick.

The pace dropped in the closing stages. In the 67th minute, Jaime López, Zelu and Rubén Mesa replaced Isma, Julito and Chuma, and in the 81st Guille Campos came on for Peregrina. Antoniano never tested Carlos Pantoja, and Xerez wrapped up a win that keeps them in the play-off places.

What Segovia said

"When we do things well we are a difficult team, but when we don't, we are an ordinary team like any other," warned the coach, who called for going "step by step". Read all his comments here.
