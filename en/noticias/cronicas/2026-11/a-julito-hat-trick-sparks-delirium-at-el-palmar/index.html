A Julito hat-trick sparks delirium at El Palmar

Julito scored three goals in 26 minutes and Jaime López added the other in a 0-4 rout of Atlético Sanluqueño at El Palmar. Xerez CD win their third match in a row, reach 15 points and remain one of the teams that concede the fewest goals in the group.

Xerez CD left El Palmar with their biggest win of the season and another clean sheet. At half-time the match was still level. In the second half Abel Segovia's side, with the wind behind them, set the pace and found their finishing: Julito opened the scoring in the 52nd minute, Jaime López got the second in the 76th and the winger completed his hat-trick with two goals in barely a minute (77' and 78'). More than a thousand Xerez fans celebrated in the stands.

A wall built on Segovia's idea

The rout shows on the scoreboard, but the team is built on its defence. Xerez have conceded only seven goals in ten matchdays, and before this round only Badajoz had let in fewer in the group. Since Abel Segovia took over after Xavi Molist was sacked, the team has quickly taken on his idea: organisation, compact lines and patience on the ball. In his six league matches they have taken 13 points out of 18 and conceded only four goals.

Fali, the only change

Segovia barely touched the side that won in Tamaraceite. Fali came into the centre of defence for Josete. Pantoja played in goal; Guille Campos, Moi, Fali and Chacartegui in defence; Raúl López and Isma in midfield, with Peregrina ahead of them; Julito and Zeki on the wings, and Chuma up front. Rubén Mesa, injured, was not in the squad. Saúl Romero was included in a squad for the first time after breaking his fifth metatarsal in pre-season.

An uphill start

Playing into the wind, Xerez struggled to get into the game. Sanluqueño won three corners in a row in the opening minutes. The first warning from the blues came in the 11th minute: Zeki met a Julito cross twice and Rubén Gálvez saved both. In the 14th, Julito, out wide, shot straight at the goalkeeper.

In the 18th minute the referee stopped the match for four minutes so that a Xerez fan who had fainted in the stands could be treated. He left the ground in a wheelchair. Xerez found it hard to get back into their rhythm. In the 29th, Salguero was through on his own against Pantoja after a lapse by Chacartegui, and the goalkeeper saved by pushing the ball out for a corner. Before half-time, Rubén Gálvez saved a low shot from Julito (41') and another from Chacartegui after a Peregrina counter (45'); Chuma sent the rebound over the bar. The referee added seven minutes and, in that time, booked Fali and Asier Benito after a tussle.

Julito breaks the game open

Segovia took off Guille Campos, who was on a yellow card, at half-time and brought on Víctor López at right back. Seven minutes later the first goal arrived: Chacartegui and Zeki combined, Gálvez saved the left-footer's shot and Julito put away the rebound (0-1, 52'). Chuma could have made it two soon after, but was denied by the goalkeeper.

Jaime López and Jaime Fernández came on in the 68th minute for Peregrina and Zeki. The change worked straight away. In the 76th, Jaime López, just on, scored with his left foot from a ball Julito played into the box (0-2). A minute later, Julito finished a Jaime López cross at the far post (0-3). In the 78th he completed his hat-trick with his right foot from inside the box (0-4). The away fans ended the match singing at El Palmar.

Looking upwards

With three wins in a row (Marbella, Tamaraceite and Sanluqueño), Xerez CD reach 15 points and, with Betis B-Tenerife B postponed, are fifth, in the play-off places. The next match is on Sunday 15 November at 18:00 against Antoniano, at the Pedro Garrido stadium.

What Segovia said

"A team that wants to attack needs to defend better. A team is built on defensive organisation", explained the coach, who called for calm despite the run: "There are 24 matchdays left to play". Read all his comments here.

Before the match a minute's silence was held in memory of Jesús de Miguel, the Tenerife forward who died this week. In the 9th minute the crowd gave him a long ovation.
