From Ultra Sherry to Hools XCD: the story of Xerez's singing section

Photo: Archive

Xerez CD's singing section has almost four decades of history. It was born in the mid-eighties at the old Domecq stadium, found its most influential group in Ultra Sherry from 1991 onwards and split in 2013 with the birth of Xerez Deportivo FC. Since then, Hools XCD have been the ones cheering on Xerez CD.

According to the histories compiled by the fans themselves, including the book Kolectivo Sur XX Aniversario: La historia de los ultras del Xerez, published in 2011, the Xerez ultras movement took off with promotion to Segunda División in the 1985/86 season.

The Domecq years

By the 1986/87 season there were already several groups at the old Domecq stadium. División Blanquiazul, made up mainly of students from the El Pilar and La Salle schools, stood in the corner between the main stand and the south end. In that same end stood a first group called Ultra Sherry, known for its flags and banners. Los Pantera completed the stand, taking their name from the Ecuadorian player "Pantera" Benítez.

The move to Chapín

With the move to Chapín in the 1988/89 season, Ultramuces was born in the south end, along with Fondo Azul. They were years of big away trips: according to the same sources, in the 1989/90 season around 1,500 Xerez fans travelled to Huelva and to the Santiago Bernabéu to watch Xerez face Castilla.

Relegation to Segunda División B in 1990/91 changed the picture. Fondo Azul and Ultramuces disappeared, and only a small group, the Dalton's, made up of former Ultramuces members, stayed on the terraces until the following season.

Ultra Sherry, the group that revolutionised Chapín

In 1991 a new Ultra Sherry was born, different from the one of the eighties, made up of young fans and with its own sections such as Cockney Sherry Crew and Youth Xerez. It was the group that, according to fans of the time, revolutionised the Chapín terraces.

The group changed its name in 1995 to Hinchada Azul. A year later, in 1996, it adopted the name Kolectivo Sur, under which it became the longest-running singing section in Xerez history. In an interview published in 2009, the group itself stated that its only politics was Xerez CD.

2013: the split

Xerez CD's financial crisis in 2013 split the fan base in two. On 28 June that year, a group of fans founded Xerez Deportivo FC, after a vote in which 75.79% backed the creation of the new club.

Kolectivo Sur put its future to an internal vote. The majority chose the new project, and in July 2013 the group announced in a statement that it would support Xerez Deportivo FC "in whatever category it plays in".

The members who did not want to leave Xerez CD formed Hools XCD. They started out with between 15 and 20 people and celebrated their fifth anniversary in 2018. Today their banner still follows the team, as seen last Sunday in the stand at the Juan Guedes during the win over Tamaraceite.
