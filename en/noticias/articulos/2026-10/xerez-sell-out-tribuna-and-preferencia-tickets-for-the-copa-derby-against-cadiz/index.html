Xerez sell out Tribuna and Preferencia tickets for the Copa derby against Cádiz

Photo: Diario Xerecista

Tickets in the Tribuna and Preferencia for the Copa del Rey first-round match against Cádiz CF, to be played on Wednesday 28 October at 21:00 at Chapín, have sold out eleven days before the match, the club has announced.

Xerez CD have sold all Tribuna and Preferencia tickets for the provincial derby against Cádiz CF. Sales opened on Thursday 15 October, with prices of 35 euros in the Tribuna and 30 in Preferencia for the general public, and 20 and 15 euros respectively for members.

Few tickets left in the Fondo Sur

Few seats remain in the Fondo Sur. They cost 25 euros for the general public and 10 for members, a price that applies until Sunday 25; from Monday 26, all tickets will be sold at the general price.

A tie against a Segunda División side

The match pits Xerez, who compete in the Segunda RFEF, against Segunda División side Cádiz CF in the first round of the Copa del Rey. Cádiz supporters have the Fondo Norte reserved for them, with tickets at 25 euros.
