Xerez - Cádiz, postponed after the Urda train crash

Photo: Diario Xerecista

The Copa del Rey tie between Xerez CD and Cádiz CF, scheduled for tonight at 21:00 at Chapín, has been postponed by the RFEF following the train crash in Urda (Toledo).

According to the information available so far, the crash has left 30 people dead and many injured.

There is still no new date for the match, which had generated huge anticipation in Jerez: Chapín was set to be almost full.

Diario Xerecista sends its condolences to the families of the victims and wishes the injured a speedy recovery.
