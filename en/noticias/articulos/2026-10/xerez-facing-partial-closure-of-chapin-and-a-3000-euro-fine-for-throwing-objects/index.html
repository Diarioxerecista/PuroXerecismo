Xerez could receive a Copa warning over objects thrown against Cádiz

Photo: Diario Xerecista

What happened in the Copa derby against Cádiz CF would not count as a repeat offence, since the Copa del Rey and the league are different competitions and the warning the club received against Ciudad de Lucena belongs to the league.

Correction: an earlier version of this article reported that Xerez CD faced the partial closure of Chapín and a 3,000-euro fine as repeat offenders for throwing objects. That is not the case. The Copa del Rey and the league are different competitions, so what happened against Cádiz would not count as a repeat offence in relation to the warning received in the league.

According to the referee's report, in the 60th minute of the Copa del Rey first-round derby (1-3), after García Pascual made it 1-2, the match referee, Sánchez Villalobos, activated phase 1 of the protocol for thrown objects. Bottles of water and soft drinks were thrown onto the pitch from a section of the stadium occupied by home supporters, without any of them hitting anyone. The crowd was warned over the public address system that, if it happened again, the match would be temporarily suspended. The game was stopped for two minutes and 30 seconds and then continued without any further incident, according to the report.

Two different competitions

In the league match against Ciudad de Lucena, on 18 October at Chapín, the club received a warning of partial closure and a 600-euro fine. As they are different competitions, what could happen now is that the Competition Committee issues another warning in the Copa, separate from the one already in place in the league.
