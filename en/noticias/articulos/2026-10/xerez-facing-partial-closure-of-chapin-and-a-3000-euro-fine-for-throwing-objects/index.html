Xerez could face partial closure of Chapín and a 3,000-euro fine over objects allegedly thrown

Photo: Diario Xerecista

The club could be sanctioned for an alleged repeat offence of objects thrown onto the pitch, following what happened in the Copa derby against Cádiz CF.

Xerez CD could be sanctioned with the partial closure of their stadium and a 3,000-euro fine for an alleged repeat offence of throwing objects.

The possible sanction would follow the Copa del Rey first-round derby against Cádiz CF, played on Thursday at Chapín (1-3). According to the referee's report, in the 60th minute, after García Pascual made it 1-2, the match referee, Sánchez Villalobos, activated phase 1 of the protocol for thrown objects, after bottles of water and soft drinks were thrown onto the pitch from a section of the stadium occupied by home supporters, without any of them hitting anyone. The crowd was warned over the public address system that, if it happened again, the match would be temporarily suspended. The game was stopped for two minutes and 30 seconds and then continued without any further incident, according to the report.

Repeat offence

It would not be the first time this season. In the league match against Ciudad de Lucena, on 18 October at Chapín, the club had already received a warning of partial closure and a 600-euro fine, and against Cádiz the incidents allegedly happened again. That possible repeat offence is what could make the punishment harsher, going from a fine to the partial closure of the ground.

It remains to be seen which area of the stadium would be affected, if that is the case, and in which match the sanction would be served. Xerez's next home game, against Antoniano, does not yet have a venue, as Chapín is being reseeded, so we will have to wait and see in the event of a partial closure.
