Copa del Rey · First round

Xerez CD will face Cádiz CF at Chapín in the first round of the Copa del Rey

Image: Diario Xerecista

The draw held in Las Rozas produced a provincial derby that will, in all likelihood, be played on Wednesday 28 October at the azulino stadium

Xerez CD now know their opponent in the first round of the Copa del Rey: it will be Cádiz CF. That was the outcome of the draw held this Monday in Las Rozas from 13:00, in which the azulinos were paired with the yellow side, just as this website anticipated in previous days. The match will be played at Chapín.

A provincial derby in the knockout tournament

The tie pits two of the most representative clubs in the province of Cádiz against each other in a clash with a clear element of rivalry. Xerez, who compete in Segunda RFEF, will have the chance to face a Segunda División side at home, as the criterion of geographical proximity that governs these pairings had already suggested.

Expected date

The match will, in all likelihood, be played on Wednesday 28 October, although the final date and kick-off time have not yet been officially confirmed. We will have to wait for the coming days to know all the details.

A tie with a special flavour

The clash comes at a time when Xerez are looking to recapture good feelings after their first league win against Tenerife B, and it offers the azulino fans the chance to enjoy a fixture of maximum appeal at their stadium, against an opponent of enormous appeal.
