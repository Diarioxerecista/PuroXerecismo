Zelu suffers a muscle tear, another significant loss for Xerez CD

Photo: Diario Xerecista

The Azulinos’ winger was injured in the 32nd minute of the match against Betis Deportivo and tests have confirmed a muscle tear

Xerez CD have a new concern in the treatment room. Zelu has a muscle tear, as confirmed after undergoing medical tests, the injury that forced him off the pitch last Saturday at the Ciudad Deportiva Luis del Sol.

An injury that was plain to see

The player put his hand to the back of his left thigh and was unable to continue. Abel Segovia had to turn to his bench before the half-hour mark and brought on Isma Gutiérrez. Xerez, who had already gone ahead through Chuma’s free-kick, ended up drawing 1-1 against the leaders.

An important piece in the new Xerez

Zelu had started both matches under Segovia, against Tenerife B and Betis Deportivo, and was shaping up as one of the most prominent players on the left flank of the new team. His absence also comes at a bad time, with two consecutive matches at Chapín against Ciudad de Lucena and Marbella.

Timescales to be determined

For the moment no recovery time has been announced, which will depend on how the injury evolves. We will have to wait for the club’s medical report to find out how many matchdays the winger will miss.
