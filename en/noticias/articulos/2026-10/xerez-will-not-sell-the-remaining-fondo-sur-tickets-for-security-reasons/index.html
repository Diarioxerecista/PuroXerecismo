Copa del Rey · Xerez CD - Cádiz CF

Xerez will not sell the remaining Fondo Sur tickets for security reasons

Photo: Diario Xerecista

Xerez CD will not put on sale the tickets left over in the Fondo Sur for the Copa del Rey tie against Cádiz CF, on Wednesday at Chapín, for security reasons, as the club's vice-president has confirmed to this outlet.

Cádiz train behind closed doors

Cádiz, who changed coach this week, will train behind closed doors ahead of the tie. Everything suggests the yellow side will give minutes to their less regular players, although they could include some of those who played against Ceuta, depending on each player's physical load.

Segovia gives nothing away

For his part, Abel Segovia gave no clues about his approach. The Xerez coach explained that he had studied the opponents, but under their previous coach. Now he will have to get down to work to analyse the yellow side again.
