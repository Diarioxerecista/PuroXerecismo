The Xerez CD crest slips into a PP video against the PSOE

Photo: Diario Xerecista

The Partido Popular has published a video aimed at Pedro Sánchez on its official X account in which the main character wears a sweatshirt bearing the Xerez CD crest.

The video lasts one minute and 29 seconds and was posted with the message "We hope the pizza doesn't upset your stomach, Pedro", together with a mention of Sánchez's account. It opens with a shot of the sign outside a PSOE office. At the time of writing, it had over 290,000 views.

The crest, front and centre

In several shots, the young man who stars in the piece is seen walking down the street with a motorcycle helmet under his arm. He is wearing a navy Luanvi sweatshirt with the Xerez CD crest on the chest.

Diario Xerecista is not aware that the club authorised the use of its crest in the video, nor that it has commented on the matter.
