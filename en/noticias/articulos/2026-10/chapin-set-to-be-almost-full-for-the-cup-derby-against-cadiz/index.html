Chapín set to be almost full for the cup derby against Cádiz

Photo: Diario Xerecista

Chapín will be close to full this Wednesday at 21:00 for the Copa del Rey tie between Xerez CD and Cádiz CF. The club has put back on sale the tickets released by season-ticket holders, in every part of the stadium. They cannot be bought at the ticket office.

Update: the match has been postponed with no new date set because of the Urda train crash.

Xerez CD sold out the Tribuna and Preferencia stands ten days ago, when only a few seats were left in the Fondo Sur. Now the tickets released by season-ticket holders are available again, spread across the whole ground.

Online only

Tickets can only be bought on the Xerez CD website, which is also selling seats in the away section. They are no longer on sale on the Cádiz CF website. The ticket offices will not sell tickets, so anyone who wants to attend the derby will have to buy them in advance.

No official figure for Cádiz fans

Cádiz CF supporters have been allocated the Fondo Norte. Because of the security operation for the match, the number of Cádiz fans travelling to Jerez will not be made public, although it could be around 2,800.
