Segunda RFEF · Matchday 10

Atlético Sanluqueño v Xerez CD will be played on Sunday 8 November at 12:30

Photo: Diario Xerecista

The match between Atlético Sanluqueño and Xerez CD, on matchday ten of Group IV of the Segunda RFEF, now has a kick-off time: it will be played on Sunday 8 November at 12:30 at El Palmar in Sanlúcar de Barrameda.

It will be Xerez's second consecutive match with a morning kick-off. A week earlier, on Sunday 1 November, they visit Tamaraceite at 13:00.

Before those two away trips, on Wednesday 28 October, the team host Cádiz CF at Chapín in the first round of the Copa del Rey.
