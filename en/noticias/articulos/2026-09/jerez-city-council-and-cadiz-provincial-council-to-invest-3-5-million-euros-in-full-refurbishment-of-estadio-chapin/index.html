Jerez City Council and Cádiz Provincial Council to invest 3.5 million euros in the full refurbishment of Estadio Chapín

Photo: Diario Xerecista

The project, first reported by David Sánchez in Diario de Jerez, includes new turf and irrigation, new LED lighting, seat renewal and accessibility improvements at a venue that had been dragging long-standing structural shortcomings

Jerez City Council and Cádiz Provincial Council will undertake a thorough modernisation of the Estadio Municipal Chapín thanks to a joint investment of 3.5 million euros, as revealed in information first reported by our colleague David Sánchez in Diario de Jerez. The announcement was confirmed by the mayor during the municipal Plenary session, accompanied by the councillor for Sport, Tomás Sampalo, who together detailed an extensive package of structural works aimed at correcting deficiencies the stadium had been dragging for years.

New playing surface and full modernisation

Among the project's most notable items is an investment of 400,000 euros for the complete renewal of the turf, a measure that had become urgent given the problems detected in the drainage system, the build-up of what is known as Black Layer, the poor filtration of rainwater and the wear of an irrigation network whose maintenance and spare parts had become financially unsustainable.

A breakdown of works covering the entire venue

In addition to the new playing surface, the approved investment plan includes a series of improvements that will affect different areas of the stadium:

LED lighting: 1,550,000 euros
Seat renewal: 1,150,000 euros
Lift installation: 250,000 euros
New dugouts: 100,000 euros
Replacement of the irrigation system with a hydraulic one: 55,000 euros
Area adapted for people with reduced mobility (PRM): 50,000 euros
New water tanks for irrigation: 30,000 euros

The mayor defends the local government's order of priorities

During her speech, the mayor of Jerez expressly thanked the president of the Cádiz Provincial Council for her involvement in launching this joint project. The mayor also used her appearance to justify the strategy followed by the municipal government before tackling an investment of this size at the Xerez ground, explaining that it had been a priority to first address the deteriorated state of the sports courts and pitches spread across the city's different neighbourhoods, before concentrating financial resources on the Chapín facilities.

A plan within a broader investment strategy

Finally, the mayor framed this work within a broader package of interventions considered priorities for the city as a whole, among which she also cited the renewal of the municipal bus fleet and the resurfacing of various public roads. The mayor also confirmed that for the 2027 financial year, already within the next term of office, Estadio Chapín will continue to receive additional investment to complement the works now announced.
