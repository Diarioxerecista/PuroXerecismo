Xerez CD close to an agreement with Abel Segovia, although the appointment is not yet finalised

Photo: Marbella CF

The coach is the board's choice to lead the azulinos until the end of the current season, but the final agreement has yet to be sealed because of the backroom team that accompanies him, made up of assistants who were already with him at Sanluqueño and Numancia

Everything points to Abel Segovia being the Xerez CD board's choice to take charge of the dugout until the end of the current season, following the dismissal of Xavi Molist last week. However, despite a decision already having been made on the coach's name, the final agreement has not yet been sealed, and the reason is none other than the backroom team that accompanies the coach, made up of assistants with whom he has previously worked at both Sanluqueño and Numancia.

A trusted coaching staff as a condition

Segovia, it has emerged, does not intend to take on the Xerez project alone, but accompanied by the same assistants who have supported him in previous spells as a coach. It is a common formula in football, where many coaches make their arrival conditional on being able to bring their own trusted team, but in this case it has become the real sticking point in the negotiations between the two parties.

The backroom team, the real obstacle

The problem, according to the information available, does not lie in the coach's sporting profile or his suitability for the role, on which there is already consensus within the board, but precisely in the conditions linked to the incorporation of his coaching staff. It is that issue, and no other, that is delaying the signing of the agreement until the end of the season that both parties are aiming to reach.

An appointment still not finalised

While this hurdle is being resolved, Xerez CD has still not made official the appointment of its new coach, with the clock ticking against them as the new coach's league debut approaches, scheduled for next Sunday against Tenerife B. The club will need to resolve this matter as soon as possible if it wants the coach to have the days needed to prepare for the match with his own coaching staff on the Chapín pitch.
