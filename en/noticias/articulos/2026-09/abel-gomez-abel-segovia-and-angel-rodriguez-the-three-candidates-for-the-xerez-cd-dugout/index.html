Club · Dugout

Abel Gómez, Abel Segovia and Ángel Rodríguez, the three candidates for the Xerez CD dugout

Photo: Diario Xerecista

Following the dismissal of Xavi Molist, the club's board is weighing up three names to lead a team that has taken two points from twelve and sits in the relegation places

Xerez CD is already working on finding a new head coach. Just hours after Xavi Molist's departure was made official, the board of directors has put forward a shortlist of candidates to take charge of the first team: Abel Gómez, Abel Segovia and Ángel Rodríguez.

A decision that cannot wait

Sporting urgency is setting the club's timetable. The Jerez side finds itself in this situation with just two points out of a possible twelve, without a win in the first four matchdays of Group 4 of Segunda RFEF and sitting in the relegation zone. The club therefore intends to resolve the succession as quickly as possible, so that the chosen coach can take charge of the squad at the earliest opportunity and have the maximum number of training sessions ahead of the next league fixture.

Three profiles on the table

The names being considered by the board of directors are Abel Gómez, Abel Segovia and Ángel Rodríguez, three coaches currently within the club's orbit, from whom the board will make its choice in the coming hours. For the time being, the club has not made public any details about its order of preference or the state of talks with each of them.

A demanding challenge for the chosen coach

Whoever finally takes the seat on the Chapín bench will inherit a dressing room in need of confidence, a team that has shown a lack of personality and little attacking output, and a fan base that has voiced its discontent in recent weeks. The new coach's first task will be to reverse the trend and return Xerez to winning ways, in a start to the season that demands immediate reactions.

This is a developing story. The club is expected to announce its final decision in the coming days.
