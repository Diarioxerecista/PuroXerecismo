The García-Delgado Group sells its stake in Minera and turns its sights on Xerez CD

Photo: CD Minera

Carlos García-Delgado steps down from his role at the Murcian club over scheduling and personal incompatibilities, and holds an initial contact with the Xerez board to sound out the reality of the club and its future intentions, according to Manuela Romero writing in Diario de Jerez

The Jerez firm García-Delgado took control of Deportiva Minera's shareholding last summer, an operation it shared with Serbian investor Micho, one of its partners in that project. Now, however, the Jerez business group has decided to cut all ties with the Murcian club, handing over its entire shareholding package to the Balkan investor.

The Llano del Beal side competed last season in Group 4 of Segunda RFEF, the same category in which both Xerez CD and Xerez DFC were placed, coming close then to a place in Primera RFEF under the orders of José Carlos Checa. This season, however, Minera have been reassigned to Group 3, meaning their paths will no longer cross with those of the Jerez club, who remain in Group 4.

Departure over a scheduling clash

Carlos García-Delgado, who held the role of the Murcian club's chief executive alongside José Blaya as its top institutional figure, has decided to step away from the project, citing professional and personal reasons, as he could no longer combine his many commitments with the constant weekly trips to Murcia.

His departure coincides with that of Quique Romero, who also carried out advisory duties for both the presidency and the sporting area of the club, acting as an institutional officer.

An agreement made public by the company itself

It was the Jerez company itself that announced these corporate moves through its website, where it confirms it has closed "a strategic agreement for the sale of the entirety of its shareholding in Club Deportivo Minera to the investment firm Terval eim Holding".

In the same statement, the Jerez firm also lets slip that its top representative recently held a meeting with Xerez CD officials, with the aim of "exploring and possibly beginning a new chapter at the club of his home city", a phrase that opens the door to possible future involvement in the blue-and-white club. The company even goes so far as to suggest that this approach could mark "the start of a key stage for the institutional stability" of the Xerez club.

Xerez CD's cautious response

Xerez CD's offices have not denied the meeting, though they have sought to play down its immediate significance. Club sources confirm the meeting held with the Jerez businessman and qualify its scope with a cautious message: "We have heard the proposal and his intentions. We listen to every businessman who knocks on our door, we don't close it to anyone", a statement that leaves the door ajar without committing, for now, to any kind of formal agreement or negotiation.
