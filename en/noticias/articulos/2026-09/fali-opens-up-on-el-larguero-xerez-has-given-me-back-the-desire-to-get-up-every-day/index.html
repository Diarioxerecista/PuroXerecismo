Fali opens up on El Larguero: "Xerez has given me back the desire to get up every day"

Photo: Diario Xerecista

The blue-and-white defender and Kiko Narváez reviewed the state of the Jerez club on Cadena SER's national programme, where the player revealed deeply personal chapters and the former pundit praised Gallo's work as sporting director

The name of Xerez CD echoed once again on national radio. El Larguero, on Cadena SER, devoted a slot to the latest on the blue-and-white side with the participation of Fali, a defender with the Jerez team, who shared with Kiko Narváez a conversation full of personal confessions and reflections on the club's present.

A life away from the media spotlight

One of the first subjects to come up was the player's absence from social media, a personal decision he explained as a way of protecting his privacy and staying clear of the noise that tends to accompany professional football these days. A quirk that drew attention on a programme with national reach, used to interviewing players with a wide digital profile.

The conversation soon turned to one of the most delicate episodes of his career: his departure from Cádiz CF. Fali did go into that chapter at length, covering details and circumstances of his exit from the yellow side that we, from here, prefer not to reproduce or pick apart.

Xerez, a lifeline

It was at that point in the interview that the player delivered the line that has travelled furthest since his appearance on national airwaves. Fali admitted he went through a really hard time back then, and that it has been precisely his arrival at Xerez CD that has given him back the desire to get up every day, in remarks charged with emotion that show just how far the blue-and-white dressing room has become a personal refuge for the player.

The defender was not short of praise for the Jerez supporters, for whom he said he gives his life in every match, underlining the special bond he has developed with a fanbase that, in his own words, has been decisive in his emotional recovery. Fali even expressed his wish to play one day in a derby between Xerez and Cádiz, a match he admitted he would especially love to experience from inside the pitch, despite the mixed feelings his past at the yellow club may stir in him.

Kiko Narváez highlights Gallo's role

During the chat, Kiko Narváez also took the chance to focus on the sporting management of the Jerez club, highlighting the great work Gallo is doing as the blue-and-white sporting director. The former Cadena SER pundit underlined the solidity of the project being built in Jerez, endorsing the sporting chief as one of the keys to the side's present and future.

An interview, in short, that served to bring the reality of Xerez CD closer to a national audience, making clear that, beyond results on the pitch, the Jerez club has become for some of its players far more than simply a professional destination.
