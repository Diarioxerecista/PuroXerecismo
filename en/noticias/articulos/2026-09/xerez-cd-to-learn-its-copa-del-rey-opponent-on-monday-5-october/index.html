Copa del Rey · Draw

Xerez CD will learn its Copa del Rey opponent this Monday, 5 October

Photo: Diario Xerecista

The draw for the first round will be held from 13:00 in the Salón Luis Aragonés at the Ciudad del Fútbol, with the possibility that the azulinos will face a side from a higher division

Xerez CD will be following closely this coming Monday, 5 October, the draw for the first round of the Copa del Rey, which will be held from 13:00 in the Salón Luis Aragonés at the Ciudad del Fútbol. The event will determine the opponent that will cross the Jerez side's path on its run in the knockout tournament, with the chance of facing a team from a higher division.

Criteria of geographical proximity and sporting level

The draw will be made according to criteria of geographical proximity between the participating clubs, while also seeking, whenever possible, to pair teams from lower divisions with sides from higher levels, in the traditional format that characterises the early rounds of the competition.

Composition pending the preliminary round

The final composition of the groups for this draw will, however, depend on how the preliminary round scheduled for 3 and 4 October is resolved, the outcome of which will determine which teams finally enter the pool of participants ahead of Monday's event.

The four Supercopa representatives, exempt from this round

FC Barcelona, Real Madrid, Atlético de Madrid and Real Sociedad, the four teams that will contest the next Supercopa de España, are exempt from playing this first round. These teams will join the competition directly in the round of 32.
