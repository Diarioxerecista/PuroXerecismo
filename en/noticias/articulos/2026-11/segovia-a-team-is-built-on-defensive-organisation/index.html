Xerez CD · Reaction

Segovia: "A team is built on defensive organisation"

Photo: Diario Xerecista

The Xerez CD coach was pleased after the 0-4 rout at El Palmar. He highlighted the clean sheet, his side's superiority in the second half and the reward for Saúl and Salas, but he does not want to look at the table: "There are 24 matchdays left".

Abel Segovia described the win over Atlético Sanluqueño as another step in the team's development. Xerez have now won three in a row, but the coach from Seville did not want to attach importance to the table at this stage of the season.

A poor start and a stoppage that did not help

Segovia admitted that his team struggled to get into the game: "The first five minutes were hard for us. We weren't ourselves and we were just waiting to see what happened". Then came a good spell on the ball, which was interrupted when the match was stopped so that a fan could be treated in the stands: "We could have gone ahead, but the stoppage because of what happened to a fan hurt us quite a lot. We couldn't wait to get to half-time because the wind made it hard for us".

Superior after the break

In the second half, with the wind behind them, Xerez dominated: "We were clearly superior, we know how to kill a game and play with the wind". Even so, the coach would have liked more of the ball: "We are a team that offers a lot in attack. But anyway, in the end I'm happy with the result".

Goals come from hard work

After a low-scoring start to the league, the team has scored seven in the last two matches. "The work is paying off. We create a lot and we weren't being clinical. Since we started to be, we have been better than our opponents", he explained. But he called for calm: "There's a long way to go. There are 24 matchdays left to play. We take a small step and our confidence in front of goal grows".

The clean sheet, the foundation

Segovia placed great value on not conceding. Xerez have let in only seven goals in ten matchdays. "For a team that wants to achieve things, it's important to keep clean sheets. A team that wants to attack needs to defend better. A team is built on defensive organisation", he said.

A reward for Saúl and Salas

Saúl and Salas played their first minutes of the season. "They need to feel important. They give me a lot and I'm going to give back to them. It's a reward for the work they are doing, and they are doing it very well", said the coach.

Rubén Mesa, on Wednesday

On Rubén Mesa's absence, Segovia explained that the striker "is injured". He felt discomfort in the cup match against Cádiz and could not play in Tamaraceite: "This week he was recovering and next Wednesday he will be training normally".
