Transfer rumours speed up Mario Peregrina's contract renewal

Photo: Diario Xerecista

The club want to extend the contract of the 22-year-old attacking midfielder from Huelva, which runs until 30 June 2027, after Espanyol B and several Primera Federación clubs are said to have shown interest in him.

Xerez CD have stepped up talks to renew Mario Peregrina. According to reports, Espanyol B and several Primera Federación sides are said to be interested in the midfielder. His current contract with the club runs until 30 June 2027.

Peregrina, born in Minas de Riotinto (Huelva) on 8 February 2004, joined Xerez on 29 July from Córdoba CF B, where he played after coming through the Real Betis academy. He wears the number 18 shirt.

A regular starter

The attacking midfielder has earned a place in Abel Segovia's side. He started the last few matches against Atlético Sanluqueño, Antoniano and Atlético Central. Against Antoniano he scored the second goal in the 44th minute, his first league goal, and in Alcalá de Guadaíra he played a fine pass to Rubén Mesa and forced Santos into a good save with a low shot.

Xerez, second in the table after five straight wins, are looking to tie down one of the young players with the most potential in their squad.
