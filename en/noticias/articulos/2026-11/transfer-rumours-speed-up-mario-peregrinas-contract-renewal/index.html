Xerez tie down Mario Peregrina with an improved contract and a higher release clause

Photo: Diario Xerecista

The club have agreed a new deal with the 22-year-old attacking midfielder from Huelva amid interest from Primera Federación sides. The move is expected to be made official in the coming hours.

Xerez CD have tied down Mario Peregrina. According to our colleague David Sánchez of Diario de Jerez, the club agreed an improved contract for the midfielder a few days ago, including a significant rise in his release clause. The renewal is expected to be announced officially in the coming hours.

The club moved quickly in response to interest from several Primera Federación sides. According to journalist Ángel García, a reserve team from Group 2 of the third tier is keeping a close eye on the player. Although the club are confident Peregrina will finish the season at Chapín, they do not rule out a higher-division side paying the clause.

A key piece under Segovia

Peregrina, born in Minas de Riotinto (Huelva) in 2004, arrived this summer from Córdoba CF B after coming through the Real Betis academy. He did not start the season in the starting line-up, but since Abel Segovia took over he has become a regular. According to figures reported by Diario de Jerez, he has made seven consecutive starts and played 680 minutes across the league and the cup. Against Antoniano he scored his first league goal, and last Sunday in Alcalá de Guadaíra he was again one of the team's best players in his 66 minutes on the pitch.

The player himself played down the rumours a few days ago at a press conference, saying he was "very calm, very happy here" and focused on "pushing upwards with Xerez".
