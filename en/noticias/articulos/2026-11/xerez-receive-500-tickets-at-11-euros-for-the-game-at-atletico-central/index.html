Xerez receive 500 tickets at 11 euros for the game at Atlético Central

Photo: Diario Xerecista

Under-12s will get in free for Sunday's Segunda RFEF Matchday 12 game, which kicks off at 12:00 in Alcalá de Guadaíra.

Xerez CD have received a first batch of 500 tickets for next Sunday's game, on 22 November, away to Atlético Central. Each ticket costs 11 euros in advance, and under-12s will get in free. The club has announced that the number of tickets could be increased depending on demand.

Tickets are on sale at the club offices, Monday to Friday, from 10:30 to 13:30 and in the afternoon from 17:00. Anyone buying at the ground on match day will pay 16 euros.

Four wins in a row

The Group IV Segunda RFEF Matchday 12 game kicks off at 12:00 at the Nuevo Estadio Ciudad de Alcalá, in Alcalá de Guadaíra. Xerez arrive in fifth place, with 18 points and four wins in a row, after beating Antoniano 2-0. Atlético Central are eleventh, with 15 points.
