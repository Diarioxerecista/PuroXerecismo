Segunda RFEF · Matchday 12

Del Río and Santos, two of Xerez's promotion heroes, face their former club with Atlético Central

Photo: Diario Xerecista

Both were part of the squad that took Xerez CD up to Segunda RFEF, and on Sunday they will be on the other side at the Ciudad de Alcalá.

Next Sunday's game between Atlético Central and Xerez CD, at 12:00 in Alcalá de Guadaíra, will have a special edge. The Seville side include Del Río and Santos, two players who took part in Xerez's promotion to Segunda RFEF.

Now they will come up against their former team in Matchday 12 of Group IV. Xerez arrive in fifth place, with 18 points and four wins in a row. Atlético Central are eleventh, with 15 points.
