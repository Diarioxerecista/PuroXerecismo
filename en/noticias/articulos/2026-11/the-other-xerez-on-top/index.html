The other Xerez on top

Photo: Xerez CD Baloncesto

While the football team fights for the play-offs in Segunda RFEF, the Xerez CD Baloncesto players are leading Liga Femenina 2 in their first season in the division. They deserve the attention of the Xerez faithful.

A fan complained a few days ago, rightly, that nobody pays any attention to Xerez women's basketball. The fan is right, and at Diario Xerecista we are going to start doing so.

Five wins in a row. Leaders of Group B of Liga Femenina 2 with five wins and just one defeat, and the best points difference among the top teams. And all of that in their debut in the division. A newly promoted side rarely arrives like this.

Promotion was no fluke. In June, at the Ruiz-Mateos and in front of their own fans, Trini Bou's team won convincingly against San Ignacio and Al-Qázeres. They lost the last game against Casablanca by a single point, but the job was already done: Xerez were in Liga Femenina 2. It came a year after falling just short.

The same badge

It is the same badge we take to El Palmar, to Tamaraceite or to Chapín. Xerez CD is not just a football team: it is a club, with a basketball section that has been growing for almost a decade and today competes in the second tier of Spanish basketball.

That is why it is hard to understand that many Xerez fans do not know their club is top of a national league. These players' wins are ours too.

Filling the stands

Football has taught us how much it is worth to have the fans close by. They are winning everything on the court. What they lack, and that is up to us, is a crowd that matches what they are achieving.

A club becomes great when all its sections feel the same support. This basketball Xerez has already done its part. Now it is our turn.
