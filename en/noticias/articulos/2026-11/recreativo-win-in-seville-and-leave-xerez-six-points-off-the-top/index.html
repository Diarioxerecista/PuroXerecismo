Recreativo win in Seville and leave Xerez six points off the top

Photo: Diario Xerecista

Recreativo de Huelva beat Sevilla Atlético 1-2 in the postponed matchday 11 fixture and pull clear at the top with 27 points, six more than Xerez CD. Abel Segovia's side, in second place, cannot afford to slip up on Sunday against Linares Deportivo at Chapín.

The Huelva side claimed their eighth win of the season and extend their lead at the top. They have 27 points from 12 matches, with the most prolific attack in the group: 30 goals scored and only 9 conceded. Xerez, with 7 goals conceded, still have the meanest defence.

Sevilla Atlético, on 14 points, drop to thirteenth, the play-out position.

A Sunday with no margin for error

Xerez are second with 21 points, level with Estepona and Don Benito. The top of the table is very tight: just two points separate second from eighth. Sunday's opponents, at 18:00 at Chapín, are one of those chasers: Linares Deportivo, sixth with 20 points, who arrive unbeaten in eight matchdays.

A slip-up at home would leave Recreativo even further ahead and put second place at risk.
