Xerez have hit the woodwork ten times in the first twelve league matchdays

Photo: Diario Xerecista

The team have struck the woodwork ten times so far this season, a figure that cost them points early in the campaign even though they are now second in the table.

Xerez CD have hit the woodwork ten times in the first twelve matchdays of Group IV of Segunda RFEF. The posts and crossbar have become regular companions for the team, denying them goals and, in some matches, points.

A start marked by the woodwork

The bad luck began on the opening matchday. In Estepona, Moi and Josete both struck the crossbar in a game that ended 0-0. Julito also hit the post, although he was offside. Against Badajoz, on matchday 2, it was Josete again who hit the bar in the 1-2 defeat. After the goalless draw with Don Benito, Xerez had already hit the crossbar five times in their first three matches without winning any of them.

On matchday 7, against Ciudad de Lucena, Chacartegui and Chuma hit the bar again in a match Xerez lost 0-1.

In the wins too

The woodwork has not gone away with the good run. On the last matchday, away to Atlético Central, Julito hit the crossbar after a deflection off a defender and Manu Galán hit the post from a corner, in a game Xerez won 0-2.

With five straight wins, Xerez are second on 21 points, three behind leaders Recreativo de Huelva. With a little more luck with those efforts, the gap to the top could be smaller.
