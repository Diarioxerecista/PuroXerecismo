Xerez CD · Injuries

Fali will undergo surgery: he will have an arthroscopy

Photo: Diario Xerecista

Xerez CD's medical report confirms that the centre-back has suffered a complex tear of the posterior horn of the lateral meniscus, affecting both surfaces of the meniscus. The 33-year-old will undergo surgery and will be out for between eight and twelve weeks.

The club has detailed the extent of the injury Fali suffered on Saturday in the final training session before the match against Atlético Central, which forced him to leave the pitch. Initial medical tests have confirmed a complex tear of the posterior horn of the lateral meniscus. Xerez's medical staff will determine the type of operation.

Arthroscopy: stitch or trim

In injuries of this kind, the decision is made by cross-checking the MRI scan against the goals for the season. The usual approach is arthroscopy, with two options: stitching the meniscus (suture) or removing only the damaged part (partial meniscectomy). The aim is to preserve the knee as much as possible and extend the player's career without compromising his performance.

The choice is no small matter. A suture preserves the meniscus, which on the lateral side is key to the stability of the knee, but it requires a longer recovery and heals less well when the tissue is already worn, something common after the age of 30. For that reason, partial meniscectomy, which allows a quicker return, is a frequent choice for footballers of Fali's age. Non-surgical treatment is only considered when the tear is stable, small and causes no symptoms on the pitch.

Out until January or February

With an absence of between eight and twelve weeks, Fali could return between mid-January and mid-February. He would miss at least the six matches up to matchday 18, at home to Estepona at Chapín, and at most up to matchday 22, away to Don Benito. Xerez host Linares Deportivo this Sunday at 18:00 without the centre-back from Valencia.
