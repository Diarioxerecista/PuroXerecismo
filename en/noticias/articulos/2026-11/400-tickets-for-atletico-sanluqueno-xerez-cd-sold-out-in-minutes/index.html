400 tickets for Atlético Sanluqueño – Xerez CD sold out in minutes

Photo: Diario Xerecista

Xerez supporters snapped up in a matter of minutes the 400 tickets Atlético Sanluqueño made available to Xerez CD for next Sunday's match at El Palmar. The tickets sold out yesterday afternoon.

Atlético Sanluqueño offered Xerez CD an allocation of 400 tickets for the matchday 10 fixture in Segunda RFEF, Group IV, to be played on Sunday 8 November at 12:30 at El Palmar. The tickets sold out within minutes on Tuesday afternoon.

The demand confirms the pull of the Xerez support, who will follow the team on a short trip to Sanlúcar de Barrameda.

Good form

Xerez go into the game on the back of two league wins in a row, the latest 1-3 away at Tamaraceite. They are tenth with 12 points, three off the play-offs. Atlético Sanluqueño are thirteenth with 10 points, in the relegation play-off spot.

A Xerez win at El Palmar could bring the team closer to the play-off places.
