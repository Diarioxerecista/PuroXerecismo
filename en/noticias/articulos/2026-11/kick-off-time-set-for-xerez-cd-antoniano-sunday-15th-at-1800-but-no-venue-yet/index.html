Xerez CD · Fixtures

Xerez will host Antoniano at the Pedro Garrido on Sunday 15th at 18:00

Photo: Diario Xerecista

Xerez CD will face Atlético Antoniano on Sunday 15 November at 18:00 at the Estadio Pedro Garrido, on matchday 11 of Segunda RFEF, Group IV. The club want to install a temporary stand to increase capacity.

The question over the venue has been resolved. The reseeding work at the Estadio Municipal de Chapín means the pitch cannot be used for 24 days, so Xerez CD will play their home game at the Pedro Garrido, the stadium Jerez City Council had offered to the city's clubs for as long as the work lasts.

A temporary stand

The club considered the capacity of the Pedro Garrido too small for the Xerez support. So that more fans can attend, Xerez want to install a temporary stand.

Before the venue was known, other options had been considered, such as El Palmar, in Sanlúcar, or swapping the order of the fixtures with Antoniano to play this game in Lebrija and the second-round meeting at Chapín.

The opponents

Antoniano arrive after thrashing Estepona 4-1 on the last matchday, a result that lifted them off the bottom. They are sixteenth with 6 points. Xerez are tenth with 12.

Before that, Xerez have another fixture: on Sunday 8th they visit Atlético Sanluqueño at El Palmar at 12:30.
