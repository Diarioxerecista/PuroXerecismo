Segunda RFEF · Matchday 12

One of the best home sides against one of the best away sides: Xerez put Atlético Central's fortress to the test

Photo: Diario Xerecista

The Alcalá de Guadaíra side have lost only one game at home, where they have conceded a single goal. Xerez CD arrive with 8 points out of 15 on the road and two away wins in a row.

Xerez CD visit the Nuevo Estadio Ciudad de Alcalá this Sunday (12:00) in a clash of contrasts: one of the strongest home sides in the division against one of the most solid away sides.

Atlético Central, newly promoted to Segunda RFEF, have won four of their five home games and conceded just one goal, the one in their only home defeat. They opened their campaign in the division with a 2-0 win over Recreativo de Huelva, the current leaders, and on matchday four beat Sevilla Atlético by the same score.

Xerez, reliable away from Chapín

Facing them will be a Xerez side that perform better away than at home. In their five trips they have picked up two wins, two draws and just one defeat, against Recreativo (1-0). They have conceded only three goals and come into the game on the back of two emphatic away wins: 1-3 at Tamaraceite and 0-4 in Sanlúcar.

Two faces of the home side

Atlético Central have earned 12 of their 15 points at home, and away from their ground they have conceded 16 of the 17 goals against them. They are eleventh, three points behind Xerez, who are fifth on 18.

The game will also be played out in the stands. Xerez have already sold the first 500 tickets and put another 500 on sale, and more than 1,000 Xerez supporters are expected in Alcalá de Guadaíra.
