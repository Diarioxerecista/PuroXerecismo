Segunda RFEF · Matchday 13

Xerez-Linares on Sunday 29 November at 18:00 at Chapín

Photo: Diario Xerecista

Xerez CD now know the kick-off time for their matchday 13 game in Segunda RFEF, Group IV: they will host Linares Deportivo on Sunday 29 November at 18:00 at the Estadio Municipal de Chapín.

It will mark Xerez's return to Chapín, where they have not played since the Copa del Rey game against Cádiz on 29 October. This Sunday's game against Antoniano on the 15th, at 18:00, is being played at the Pedro Garrido. After that, on Sunday 22, the blue-and-whites visit Atlético Central at 12:00.
