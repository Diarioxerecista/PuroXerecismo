Xerez CD · Abel Segovia press conference

Abel Segovia "super proud" of a team that covers for absences

Photo: Diario Xerecista

The Xerez CD coach feels the 0-2 win over Atlético Central flattered the hosts, praises his side for covering four absences and hails the more than 1,000 fans who travelled to Alcalá de Guadaíra.

Abel Segovia left the Ciudad de Alcalá very pleased with Xerez CD's fifth win in a row. "I think we were superior from start to finish," the coach summed up. His only complaint was the opponents' set pieces, where he asked for "a bit more calm" to avoid giving away fouls.

The key points

Better away than at home?

Xerez once again have more points away from Chapín (11) than at home (10), as in previous seasons. Segovia denies the team feel more comfortable on the road: "Away or at home, it makes no difference to me. The team always has to be the same, a recognisable team, a team with an identity." He pointed to the game against Ciudad de Lucena, "one of the most complete" his side have played, which they lost. Even so, "the more points we take away from home, the better", because at home Xerez are going to be "very strong".

Two left-backs to give Salas minutes

In the closing stages he paired Chacartegui and Salas on the left, just as he did last week on the right with Víctor López and Guille Campos. Segovia explained that he wanted to give Salas minutes because he is working very well, and that he moved Zelu to the other flank.

The result

Given what we saw, of course it's a narrow scoreline

With more accuracy, he believes Xerez "would have killed the game off earlier" and been more comfortable.

The absences

We had four players missing and they covered for them perfectly

He said he was "super proud" of every player. Only Chacartegui kept his place in defence from the Antoniano game, and the team kept a third clean sheet in a row.

Rubén Mesa

That's why I didn't take him off, because the only thing missing was a goal

The coach believes the striker "has freed himself up" and hopes he scores soon, because "it would be a release for him".

Bittor

So he sees that in the end hard work pays off

Víctor Isuskiza, who had barely played since Segovia arrived, was given minutes as a reward: "He is training very well, he is very professional."

Fali

That's why we have four or five centre-backs

The centre-back, injured in the last training session, will have to undergo tests: "He felt something and we didn't want to take a risk with him."

Linares

This has only just begun

He highlighted the value of winning in Sanlúcar and in Alcalá, where, he recalled, only Linares, the next opponents, had won: "On Sunday, with our people, we go for them from the first minute."

The fans

Seeing your people cheer you on, push you on, is the best

He thanked the thousand or so Xerez fans who filled the Preferencia stand and set a challenge: "This year we have to fill the ground, because we have to achieve good things."
