Xerez CD · Injuries

Fali tears the meniscus horn in his right knee and will be out for eight to twelve weeks

Photo: Diario Xerecista

The Xerez CD centre-back will undergo surgery in the coming days and could miss between six and ten league matches, in the middle of the team's run of five straight wins.

Fali has torn the horn of the meniscus in his right knee. The Xerez CD centre-back will undergo surgery in the coming days and will be out for between eight and twelve weeks.

The 33-year-old defender from Valencia was injured in the final training session before the match against Atlético Central and was left out of the squad. After the game, Abel Segovia explained that the player "felt something" and that the coaching staff did not want to take any risks with him.

Out until mid-January or February

Based on that timescale, Fali could return between mid-January and mid-February. In the best-case scenario he would miss the six matches up to matchday 18, at home to Estepona at Chapín. In the worst case, his absence would last until matchday 22, away to Don Benito.

It is a significant setback for Segovia, who has relied on Fali as one of his regulars at the heart of the defence. The coach has other options in that area: as he said himself on Sunday, the team have "four or five centre-backs". Xerez host Linares Deportivo on Sunday at 18:00, with Fali definitely out and Chuma still being assessed.
